Adapted from the "Midnight Finance" style.

Replit Prompt Addition: "This is the only light-themed variant. It must feel clinical and calm. Use plenty of white space and elegant, simple charts. The focus is on trust and safety. Use 'Deep Navy' for text to ensure it looks professional and easy to read".

Global Design Rules for all 4 Variants:
To ensure the "solid" look you requested, make sure your Replit prompt always includes these rules from your reference:


Mobile-First: Always 390px width (iPhone 15 Pro size).


Floating Navigation: Bottom nav must be pill-shaped, not a flat bar.


Interactive Data: Charts must be colorful and prominent—never hidden.


Irish Localisation: Ensure the app displays prices in Euro (€) and supports Revolut Pay as a primary payment option.
